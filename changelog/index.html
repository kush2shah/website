October 2026

  • Rebuild the site on Astro: every page is real HTML now, with its own link preview

  • Icebergs, from Bondi, won the UNC Global Affairs Spotlight in the 2025–26 Carolina Global Photography Competition

  • Photos hosted on Cloudinary show their camera, lens and settings too
