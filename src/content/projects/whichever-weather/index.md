Whichever Weather aggregates five weather sources — Apple WeatherKit, Google, NOAA, OpenWeatherMap, and Tomorrow.io — into one normalized model, with side-by-side forecast comparison built in Swift Charts.

I built it in SwiftUI on an MVVM architecture, with a lightweight caching proxy backend.
