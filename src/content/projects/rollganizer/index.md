Rollganizer is a native macOS app that helps photographers track their editing progress across photo collections.

As the sole creator and developer, I built it from the ground up in SwiftUI. Rollganizer scans your photo directories and detects which RAW files have been edited — checking for XMP sidecars, format conversions, and version numbering — giving you a clear view of what's left to edit.
