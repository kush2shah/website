Fotodex is a mobile app designed to help photographers make sense of their gear and what it can do.

Rather than manually searching through databases, Fotodex scans your photo metadata to automatically identify equipment, build a personal gear collection, and discover compatible lenses. I led product vision, defined the MVP, conducted market research, and collaborated closely with developer Sam Gilmore throughout prototyping.
