Centible is a spending tracker designed to help people build financial literacy.

As Team Lead and Product Manager, I led a team of 18 student developers, designers, product managers and marketers — driving Alpha and Beta testing, defining the MVP, setting team priorities, and launching on the App Store using a modified Agile process built around a safe, welcoming environment where all voices are valued.
